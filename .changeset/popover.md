Add `Popover`, a non-modal panel anchored to a trigger. It is a named `role="dialog"` that traps no focus and locks no scroll, with controlled and uncontrolled `open`, `placement` and `align` that flip and shift to stay in the viewport, Escape and outside-press dismissal, and focus returned to the trigger. The panel is portalled out of clipping ancestors and inherits the trigger's theme scope and direction.
