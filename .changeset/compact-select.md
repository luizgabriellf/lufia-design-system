Select: add `size="sm"`, a compact box for toolbars and filter rows that sizes to its content, and `variant="pill"`, a fully rounded box. Both keep the room for the chevron, so text still ellipsizes before it, and the compact box grows to the touch target on phone widths and coarse pointers. A compact select needs an `aria-label` or a visually hidden label.
