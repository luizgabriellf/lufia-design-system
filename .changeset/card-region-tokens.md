`Card` takes `as` (`div`, `section`, `article`) and `titleId`, so a card can be a region named by its own title. Its root carries `data-lufia-part="card"`. New component tokens, each falling back to today's value: `--lufia-card-radius`, `--lufia-card-overflow`, `--lufia-card-body-color`, `--lufia-card-body-font-size` and `--lufia-card-body-line-height`.
