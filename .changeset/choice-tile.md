Add `ChoiceTile`, a pressable, selectable tile for picking one value from a short visible set laid out in a row, a wrapping grid or a scrolling strip, such as a day, a time slot or a plan. It has an optional eyebrow and description, `solid` and `subtle` selected emphasis, `sm` and `md` sizes with a touch-sized target, and `fullWidth`.
