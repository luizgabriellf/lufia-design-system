<Section id="overview">

A small inline loading ring for work of unknown length. It takes the colour of the text around it, comes in three sizes
and stands still under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

<Canvas of={Stories.Sizes} />

### Usage

```tsx
import { Spinner } from "@lufia/design-system/components/Spinner";

<Spinner label="Loading results" />;

<p>
  <Spinner size="sm" /> Loading projects…
</p>;
```

Strings in the snippet are plain literals. In your app they arrive through props from whatever copy or i18n layer you use; the package has none.

The package's own busy states (`SearchInput`, `Combobox`, `CommandPalette` and `PendingSubmit`) draw the same ring.

<Section id="accessibility">

### Accessibility

- With a `label` the spinner is `role="status"` and its text is read by assistive technology. Mount it when the work starts,
  or put the label on a region that already exists, so the announcement is reliable.
- Without a `label` it is `aria-hidden`. Use that when something else already says the page or control is busy: a
  button with `aria-busy` and its own text, or a line such as "Loading projects…" beside the ring.
- Never rely on the ring alone to say that work is in progress: it carries no text of its own.
- Under `prefers-reduced-motion` the ring stops turning and keeps its arc, so the state still reads. Under forced colours
  the track and the arc use system colours.
- The ring is a visual size of 16, 20 or 24px and is not interactive, so it has no target size.

</Section>

<Section id="guidelines">

### When to use

- A short, indeterminate wait inside a control, a row or a panel.

### When not to use

- A known amount of progress (use `ProgressBar`) or the shape of a page that is loading (use the skeletons).
- A page that must not be used until the work is done: use a modal pattern or `aria-busy` on the region.

### Content guidelines

`label` is the localised state, in the present tense: "Loading results", not "Please wait".
